Crop Disease Management

Blast (Rice)

A fungal disease caused by Magnaporthe oryzae that affects rice plants, causing lesions on leaves, stems, and panicles, leading to reduced yields.

Download PDF

Early Blight (Tomato)

Caused by Alternaria solani, this disease results in dark, sunken lesions on leaves, stems, and fruits of tomato plants, leading to premature leaf drop and reduced fruit quality.

Download PDF

Powdery Mildew (Various Crops)

A fungal disease caused by different fungi, including Erysiphe species, characterized by a white, powdery coating on leaves and stems, leading to reduced photosynthesis and plant growth.

Download PDF

Bacterial Blight (Rice)

Caused by Xanthomonas oryzae, this bacterial disease leads to water-soaked lesions on rice leaves and can cause significant yield losses if not controlled.

Download PDF

Leaf Spot (Cotton)

Caused by various fungi like Cercospora species, leaf spot causes small, dark spots on cotton leaves, which can lead to defoliation and decreased fiber quality.

Download PDF

Rust (Wheat)

Caused by Puccinia species, rust diseases (such as stem rust, leaf rust) create pustules on wheat leaves, stems, and heads, reducing grain yield and quality.

Download PDF

Mosaic Disease (Various Crops)

Caused by different viruses (e.g., Cucumber Mosaic Virus), mosaic disease results in mottled or patterned leaves, stunted growth, and reduced crop yields.

Download PDF

Downy Mildew (Various Crops)

Caused by fungi like Peronospora species, this disease results in yellowing and death of leaves and can lead to significant yield losses in crops like grapes and cucumbers.
